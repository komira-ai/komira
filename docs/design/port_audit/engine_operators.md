## Gaps

1. COUNT(DISTINCT x) and SELECT DISTINCT: served. Earlier files: agg_count_distinct.mojo, compiler_int_count_distinct.mojo. Lowering cuts PLAN_DISTINCT in src/lowering/cut_distinct.mojo and the pipeline_exec aggregation sinks fold COUNT(DISTINCT) through src/pipeline_exec/count_distinct.mojo. The kernels in src/op_agg_distinct stay a library, unwired.
2. pysdk-engine-extended-aggregates: aggregate functions beyond SUM/COUNT/MIN/MAX/AVG over ints and decimals. Earlier files: agg_extended_grouped.mojo, columnar_agg_map.mojo, agg_dict.mojo. Missing in pipeline_exec (float SUM/AVG/MIN/MAX are served, grouped and ungrouped): string MIN/MAX, MEDIAN, STDDEV/VAR, CORR, LARGEST_K, FIRST/LAST, bool aggregates. Kernels exist in src/agg and src/op_agg_slab (SlabHashAggSinkStruct). A DataFrame user sums and averages doubles and strings routinely and must match DuckDB. Task: pysdk-engine-extended-aggregates.
3. pysdk-engine-join-inner: inner/outer/semi/anti joins. Earlier files: compiler_join_chunked.mojo, compiler_join_fused.mojo, compiler_parallel.mojo (join parts). Gather and probe kernels exist (src/op_fused_kernels, src/join_assembly, src/op_join_build, src/op_join_probe) but pipeline_exec has no build segment or probe operator; 20 of 22 TPC-H queries need a join (docs/design/lowering_and_pipeline.md section 7.3 step 3). Task: pysdk-engine-join-inner.
4. pysdk-engine-cast-varchar: CAST(x AS VARCHAR) and string output. Earlier file: cast_to_varchar.mojo. Kernels exist in src/kernels/cast_to_varchar_kernels.mojo; no operator in pipeline_exec and lowering refuses a cast to string. Needed for SQL casts and DataFrame astype(str). Task: pysdk-engine-cast-varchar.
5. pysdk-engine-group-keys-mixed: GROUP BY over two or more columns of mixed types (for example int plus string) is served by the COMPOSITE row (src/pipeline_exec/sink_string_group_agg.mojo with composite; design step 8). Earlier files: composite_key_consumer.mojo, composite_key_state.mojo. Remaining: a key column of INT8, INT16, timestamp or binary is LOWERING_SINK_UNSERVED, and the partitioned sink of D8 is not built (serial per-worker merge). Task: pysdk-engine-group-keys-mixed.

## Gaps

1. Wire joins into lowering and pipeline_exec (pysdk-engine-join-inner). Earlier files: stage_primitives/join_build.mojo, join_build_stage.mojo, byte_join_build_table.mojo, byte_join_build_table_3i64pay.mojo, join_probe_borrowed_state.mojo, probe_stage.mojo, q21_parallel_mark_counter.mojo. Needed: joins are in 20 of the 22 TPC-H queries and in nearly every DataFrame merge; the design is lowering_and_pipeline.md 3.6 and 7.3 step 3.
2. Window functions are wired: lowering cuts a window node into a SINK_PARTITION_BY segment (src/lowering/cut_window.mojo) and real_builder builds a WindowSink over src/op_window (src/pipeline_exec/sink_window.mojo). Earlier file: partition_str_eval.mojo (and the window half of the partition breakers). Missing: partition top-N and window keys or arguments of the types lowering refuses by name. Needed: SQL window breadth and DataFrame rolling/rank/shift operations.
3. Wire general sort, top-N, UNION (pysdk-engine-topn-limit-offset).
