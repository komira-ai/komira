# Test 49: assert level, defines and memory cap

The checks of [test 49](README.md#49-assert-level-defines-and-memory-cap) (and of the time limit of a
`mojo_test` under `buck2 test`, [Time limits](../mojo/README.md#time-limits)), run by
[`assert_level_tests.sh`](assert_level_tests.sh). The attributes are described in
[the Mojo rules' README](../mojo/README.md#assert-level-defines-and-memory-cap).

| check | what it proves | the defect planted to see it go red |
|---|---|---|
| [`assert_level.sh`](functional/assert_level.sh) | read from `buck2 aquery` (analysis only): `-D ASSERT=none` and `-D KOMIRA_PROBE_DEFINE=on` in the compile commands of the targets that set them (a library's test build, a mojo_test, a binary and its `[shared]` library); a library's gated test at `ASSERT=none` runs under `mem_cap.sh` at 4096 MiB, at `test_memory_cap_mib = 1024` at 1024, and at `0` uncapped; and no `-D` and no `mem_cap.sh` in any command of the targets that set nothing, the examples among them; with `-c komira.coverage=true`, a library's coverage build of its test (`mojo_build_cov_test`) and its branch coverage bitcode (`mojo_emit_cov_bc`) carry the same `-D ASSERT=none` and define, and one that sets nothing has no `-D` in either; `lib_level_and_defines` (`ASSERT=none` and two defines) has the whole ordered list `-D ASSERT=none -D KOMIRA_PROBE_DEFINE=on -D KOMIRA_PROBE_SECOND=2` right after `--target-cpu` in its test build, coverage build and bitcode; and every library's bitcode has exactly the `-D` list of its coverage build; from `buck2 audit providers`, the command `buck2 test` runs for a `mojo_test` at `ASSERT=none` is `test_deadline.sh` around `mem_cap.sh` at 4096 MiB around `gate_runner.sh`, `negative:test_default`'s is `test_deadline.sh` straight around `gate_runner.sh`, and the examples' `test_hellopkg` has no `mem_cap.sh` | the `-D` arguments dropped from `mojo build` (`_build_executable`); the cap prefix always empty (`capped_prefix`); the deadline prefix dropped (`deadline_prefix`); the defines dropped from the coverage build (the `mojo_build_cov_test` call); the defines dropped from the branch coverage bitcode (`coverage_branch`'s `mojo_emit_cov_bc` command, as it first was: `lib_none mojo_emit_cov_bc: 1 command(s) do not match ', -D, ASSERT=none,'`, and `lib_defines`'s); only the first `-D` pair kept in the bitcode (`defines[:2]`: `lib_level_and_defines mojo_emit_cov_bc -D [ -D ASSERT=none], its mojo_build_cov_test [ -D ASSERT=none -D KOMIRA_PROBE_DEFINE=on -D KOMIRA_PROBE_SECOND=2]`, and its row); a `mojo_test`'s level not passed to `memory_cap` (no default cap) |
| [`functional/mem_cap:cases`](functional/mem_cap/cases.sh) | `mem_cap.sh` on a stand-in for `gate_runner.sh` whose child sleeps: it passes the runner's status through; when `/proc` stops being readable (a busybox whose `cat` of `/proc` fails after 10 readings) it exits 2 with `mem_cap: cannot read the process tree of ... killed it rather than run it uncapped`, and neither the runner nor its child is alive 5 s later; on SIGTERM (exit 143) and on SIGKILL (exit 137, through the tether) neither is alive 5 s later; a sleeping child reparented out of the runner's process tree (but still in its session) is not alive 5 s after a normal exit (`daemon_normal`) or after the cap, at 8 MiB, killed the runner's 40 MB child (`daemon_capped`, exit 137, `MEMORY CAP: killed ...`) | a failed reading taken for an exited command (it waits for the runner, uncapped, until the case's timeout); the session dropped (`setsid`); the tether dropped; the group kill after the command exits dropped (`daemon_capped`: the cap's kill by parent pid also kills the tether, so the reparented child outlives the cap) |
| [`functional/assert_level`](functional/assert_level/BUCK) | builds: at `ASSERT=none` neither of the library's `debug_assert`s is compiled into its test (`lib_none`), an `assert_mode="none"` one is not at the default level (`lib_default`), a define reaches the test file and the library's package (`lib_defines`), the level and both defines reach `lib_level_and_defines`'s test; `bin_none[run_check]` prints past its safe assert and `buck2 test :test_none` passes; with `-c komira.coverage=true`, the branch coverage runs `[coverage][branch][<test>]` of `lib_none`, `lib_defines` and `lib_level_and_defines` pass, so their bitcode was compiled at the test's level with its defines | the `-D` arguments dropped: `lib_none`, `bin_none` and `test_none` fail on their safe asserts; the defines dropped from the bitcode: the branch runs of `lib_none` (`ASSERT_PROBE safe_probe`, exit 132), `lib_defines` and `lib_level_and_defines` fail; only the first `-D` pair kept (`defines[:2]`): `lib_level_and_defines`'s fails (`-D KOMIRA_PROBE_DEFINE read in the library's package`, left `unset`) |
| [`negative/assert_level`](negative/assert_level/BUCK) | the twins fail, naming the assert: `lib_default`'s test at `ASSERT=all` (`lib_all`: `ASSERT_PROBE all_only_probe`), `lib_none`'s at the default level (`lib_safe_default`: `ASSERT_PROBE safe_probe`), the program of `bin_none` and `test_none` at the default level (`bin_default[run_check]`, `buck2 test :test_default`: exit 132); and each inadmissible declaration fails analysis with its message (an unknown level on a library or a binary, `ASSERT` in the defines, a define name that is not an identifier, a name twice, a negative cap) | the `-D` arguments dropped: `lib_all` builds |
| [`functional/mem_cap`](functional/mem_cap/BUCK) | a test holding 256 MiB for 2 s passes under a 1024 MiB cap | |
| [`negative/mem_cap`](negative/mem_cap/BUCK) | a test that allocates 64 MiB chunks without a bound of its own is killed past its 512 MiB cap, as a library's gated test (`GATED TEST FAILED: ... (exit 137)` and `MEMORY CAP: killed ... over its cap of 512 MiB`) and under `buck2 test`; the 256 MiB test under a 192 MiB cap is killed after it has started. Each holds its memory for longer than the cap's 0.1 s sampling interval (the unbounded test holds its 2 GiB fuse for 5 s, then exits 0), so a kill does not depend on timing, and a cap that does not act shows as a build that succeeds | the cap prefix always empty, and `mem_cap.sh` comparing against 64 times the cap: every target here builds |
| [`functional/test_deadline:cases`](functional/test_deadline/cases.sh) | `test_deadline.sh` on a stand-in for `gate_runner.sh` whose "test" (a copy of busybox, so not busybox's executable) sleeps: the runner's status passes through within 10 s at a 30 s limit (the timer does not hold the script); at a 2 s limit the test is killed, the runner lives to print `runner saw exit 137`, and the script exits 137 with `TEST TIME LIMIT: killed ... after 2 s, under the test runner's timeout of 62 s`; a child reparented out of the runner's tree is not alive 5 s after a normal exit; on SIGTERM (exit 143) and SIGKILL (exit 137, through the tether) neither runner nor test is alive 5 s later; a limit of 0 is refused (exit 2) | the kill of the test by executable dropped, leaving only the group kill (`expired`: the runner dies unreported); the tether dropped (`kill`). Dropping the group kill after the command exits is not caught on its own: at a normal exit the tether kills the same group (`daemon` fails only with both gone) |
| [`negative/test_deadline`](negative/test_deadline/BUCK) | `buck2 test -c komira.test_timeout_s=90 ... -- --timeout 90` of a `mojo_test` that sleeps 180 s and then exits 0: it fails with `GATED TEST FAILED: ... (exit 137)`, its own output (`TEST_DEADLINE_FIXTURE start`) and `TEST TIME LIMIT: killed ... after 30 s, under the test runner's timeout of 90 s`; and `-c komira.test_timeout_s=abc` and `=60` are refused when its BUCK file loads | the deadline prefix dropped (`deadline_prefix` returning nothing): the test runner's timeout at 90 s ends it as a bare `Fail`, with no `TEST TIME LIMIT` line and no output |
